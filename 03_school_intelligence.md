# School Intelligence

## Purpose

Transform raw research into strategic school intelligence.

Do NOT describe the school.

Explain the school.

---

# School Identity Summary

Generate a concise profile.

Include

- School Name
- Board
- Location
- Type
- Size
- Positioning

Then answer:

"What kind of school is this?"

Maximum

150 words.

---

# Educational DNA

Analyze the school deeply.

Answer:

What does this school believe?

How do they define learning?

How do they define teaching?

How do they define success?

What kind of student are they trying to build?

What kind of teacher are they trying to build?
