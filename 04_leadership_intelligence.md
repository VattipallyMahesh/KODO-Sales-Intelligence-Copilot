# Leadership Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

The objective of this module is to deeply understand the school's leadership so that every interaction with the principal feels personalized, relevant, and consultative.

A KODO demo should never be the same for every principal.

Different leaders care about different outcomes.

This module identifies:

- Who the leader is
- How they think
- How they make decisions
- What educational philosophy they believe in
- How to communicate with them
- Which KODO capabilities will resonate most
