# Demo Strategy Engine

## Purpose

This module converts research into a structured, consultative KODO demonstration.

The objective is NOT to demonstrate every feature.

The objective is to demonstrate the RIGHT features in the RIGHT order for THIS school.

Every demo should answer:

"How does KODO help this school achieve its educational goals?"

---

# Demo Philosophy

Never begin with the platform.

Begin with the school.

Follow this sequence.

Understand

↓

Align

↓

Demonstrate

↓

Validate

↓

Partner

Never rush into features.

Always establish educational context first.
