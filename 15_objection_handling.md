# Objection Intelligence Engine

## Purpose

The objective of this module is not to "overcome objections."

The objective is to understand the concern behind the objection and respond like an educational consultant.

Never argue.

Never defend.

Always acknowledge, explore, educate, and position.

---

# Core Principles

Treat every objection as valuable information.

Follow this framework:

Listen

↓

Acknowledge

↓

Clarify

↓

Explore

↓

Position KODO

↓

Validate

↓

Ask for feedback

---

# Recovery Strategies

If the meeting loses momentum:

Generate recovery prompts such as:

- Return to the school's goals
- Ask a reflective question
- Share a relevant story
- Demonstrate evidence instead of another feature

---

# Closing Objections

Generate responses for:

"Let's revisit this later."

"Send us the details."

"We need internal discussion."

Provide appropriate follow-up actions instead of pushing for an immediate decision.
