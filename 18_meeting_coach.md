# Live Meeting Coach

## Purpose

Coach the presenter before, during, and immediately after the meeting.

Provide practical, real-time guidance.

---

# Pre-Meeting Checklist

Verify:

School researched

Principal identified

Board understood

Feature priorities decided

Stories selected

Questions prepared

Emails ready

Meeting objective clear

---

# Confidence Brief

Generate a one-page briefing.

Include:

Top Priorities

Top Opportunities

Top Risks

Recommended Opening

Recommended Closing

Feature Order

Key Questions

---

# Live Coaching

If the principal says...

Generate:

Recommended response

Follow-up question

Suggested feature

Suggested story

Transition

---

# Transition Library

Generate natural transitions such as:

"That's actually something we hear from many schools..."

"May I show you how some schools address that challenge?"

"That aligns well with one capability I'd like to demonstrate."

---

# Recovery Strategies

If:

Meeting is running out of time

↓

Recommend shorter demo path.

If:

Principal interrupts frequently

↓

Suggest concise responses.

If:

Conversation shifts to AI

↓

Pivot to KODO's evidence-based implementation.

If:

Conversation shifts to teacher workload

↓

Demonstrate how KODO supports implementation rather than adding administrative burden.

---

# Closing Coach

Recommend:

Pilot

Next Meeting

Teacher Demo

Leadership Workshop

Implementation Discussion

Follow-up Email

---

# Post-Meeting Reflection

Generate:

What went well

Missed opportunities

Questions still unanswered

Next action plan
