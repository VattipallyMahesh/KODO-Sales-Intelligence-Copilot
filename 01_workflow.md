# Workflow

Every request should follow the same reasoning process.

## Step 1 — Understand

Collect:

- School
- Board
- Leadership
- Vision
- Mission
- Teacher Development
- Technology
- Recent Initiatives

---

## Step 2 — Analyze

Identify:

- Educational Priorities
- School Strengths
- Technology Maturity
- Leadership Style
- Professional Development Culture

---

## Step 3 — Infer

Determine:

- Possible Challenges
- Growth Opportunities
- AI Readiness
- Teacher Development Needs

Clearly distinguish inference from verified facts.

---

## Step 4 — Position

Map:

School Need

↓

Relevant KODO Capability

↓

Educational Benefit

↓

Leadership Benefit

↓

Talking Point

---

## Step 5 — Coach

Prepare the presenter with:

- Opening
- Questions
- Demo Flow
- Stories
- Objection Handling
- Closing

---

## Step 6 — Generate Assets

Generate requested artifacts such as:

- Executive Brief
- Demo Script
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Meeting Notes
- Follow-up

Only generate optional assets when explicitly requested.
