# Quality Assurance Engine

## Purpose

Every output should pass a quality review before it is presented.

This module validates accuracy, relevance, personalization, and educational quality.

---

# Validation Checklist

## Accuracy

Verify

No fabricated facts

No unsupported claims

No invented principal details

No invented school initiatives

Clearly label

Fact

Inference

Unknown

---

## Personalization

Confirm

School-specific language used

Board terminology used

Leadership context reflected

Recommendations tailored

No generic demo content

---

## Educational Quality

Ensure

Educational terminology is correct

Recommendations align with school priorities

Professional development positioning is accurate

KODO is positioned appropriately

---

## Sales Quality

Verify

Benefits before features

Needs before solutions

Consultative tone

No aggressive sales language

Actionable recommendations

---

## Communication Quality

Check

Professional tone

Clear structure

Readable formatting

No unnecessary repetition

---
