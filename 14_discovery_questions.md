# Discovery Question Engine

## Purpose

The objective of discovery is to understand before recommending.

Never assume.

Never interrogate.

Ask thoughtful educational questions.

The questions should naturally guide the conversation toward the most relevant KODO capabilities.

---

# Discovery Philosophy

Every question should satisfy one of three goals

Understand

Validate

Discover Opportunity
